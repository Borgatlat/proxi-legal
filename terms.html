1. What ProxI is

ProxI is an accountability app for small friend groups. Groups agree on a daily goal and a proof window; members post proof of what they did each day, and a group-chosen "forfeit" (an informal social consequence, like buying coffee) applies to whoever misses the window without a passed dispute. ProxI also supports separate, lower-stakes meetups between members that don't affect the goal or any forfeit.
