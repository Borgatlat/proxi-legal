How it works

Three steps from "we should" to "see you there".

Start a group and share its invite code, or add friends in person by trading a code on your phones.

Show up for each other

Everything inside

A group that keeps you going. Friends you actually see.

Two halves of one app, with the same people in both.

Your group

Stick with it, because your friends are watching.

  • Agree a goal and a daily window together.
  • Post a photo, video or note as proof of what you did.
  • No peeking. Everyone else's proof unlocks when yours goes up.
  • Miss the window and owe the forfeit, like buying the coffee.
  • Fair when life happens. Dispute a miss, and two thirds of your group can waive it.
Your friends

Make plans that turn into real time together.

  • "I'm free now" in one tap, or pick a time later in the week.
  • Sort the details in a message thread that belongs to the plan.
  • Hear when a friend is free, for the friends you choose.
  • A list of real friends. Online adds are capped at ten, and everyone else you add in person.
  • Up to five groups, for the gym crew, the book club and everyone in between.

Privacy

Your friends, not an audience.

ProxI is for people you know, so it is built to show as little as possible and never more than you chose.

No ads, ever

No advertising, no ad identifiers, and your data is never sold.

No analytics or tracking

Questions

Good to know.

Do my friends need ProxI too?

Yes, and that is the point. ProxI is built for people you already know, so it gets better with every friend you bring. Start a group and share its invite code, or trade codes in person.

Is this another social media app?

No. It is built around a small group and the friends you have met. There are no ads, and online friend adds are capped at ten, so your list stays people you actually know.

Does a forfeit cost real money?

Only if your group decides it should. A forfeit is whatever your group picks, like buying the next coffee. ProxI never handles money.

What if I miss a day for a good reason?

Dispute it. Your group votes, and if two thirds agree, the forfeit is waived.

Will people know if I want to see them and they don't feel the same?

Never. They only find out you marked them if they mark you too. Otherwise nobody is told, and there are no counts or hints.

Can I use it on Android?

ProxI is on iPhone first. The beta runs through TestFlight, and we will let you know about other devices.
